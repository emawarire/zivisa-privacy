# Zivisa Digital Messaging Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 19 September 2026**

Zivisa Digital Messaging (PVT) LTD ("Zivisa", "we", "us", or "our") provides digital business messaging services, including WhatsApp-based notifications, customer communications, transaction alerts, and related messaging services.

We respect the privacy of individuals whose information is processed through our services.

## Information We Process

Depending on the service being provided, we may process information such as:

- Name
- Mobile telephone number
- Email address
- WhatsApp identifiers
- Message content and messaging metadata
- Message delivery and read status
- Customer communication preferences
- Transaction or notification information supplied by businesses using our platform

We only process information necessary to provide, secure, operate, and improve our messaging services.

## How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

- Deliver WhatsApp messages and notifications
- Process business and transactional communications
- Provide customer-service communications
- Track message delivery and status
- Maintain customer communication preferences
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
- Maintain security and reliability of the platform
- Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

## WhatsApp and Meta

Zivisa uses the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta.

Information required to deliver WhatsApp communications may therefore be transmitted to and processed by Meta and other authorized technology or infrastructure providers involved in delivering the service.

## Consent and Communication Preferences

Businesses using Zivisa are expected to communicate with customers in accordance with applicable consent, opt-in, and messaging requirements.

Recipients may request that applicable communications stop or update their communication preferences.

## Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the messaging service, deliver communications, maintain infrastructure, provide security, or comply with legal obligations.

## Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

## Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to provide our services, resolve disputes, maintain security, and meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.
